Playing as

    The library is closed. Sneak through the stacks, grab every book marked on the floor, then slip out the door once it opens.

    Arrow keys or WASD to move (hold to keep going). On a phone, swipe to set a direction; tap to stop. Shelves block the librarians' flashlights, so use them as cover. One catch and the night's over.

    Each floor has a speed clock (the bar under the score): reach the door before it runs out for a bonus of 10 points per second left. Each floor adds another librarian. You enter your name once; only a new personal best updates the board.
