Playing as

    Arrow keys, WASD or swipe to steer the strand. Collect the yarn skeins (the twisted hanks with a little label band) to grow; each skein adds its colorway to the strand. Hitting a shelf, the edge or your own strand ends the run. Every 5 skeins, the yarn speeds up. Fill the whole room and you win, with a 50-point bonus.

    You enter your name once; only a new personal best updates the board.
