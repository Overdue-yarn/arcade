Playing as

    A returned book appears at the top. Slot it into the shelf in correct Dewey order before the timer runs out.

    Left / right moves the arrow between books; space shelves it. On a phone, tap the gap where it goes.

    How call numbers sort: first the 3-digit number, then the decimal digit by digit (746.4 comes before 746.43, which comes before 746.5), then the author letters A to Z. Each new section gets tighter: different hundreds, then the same hundred, then decimals, then same number sorted by author.

    Three misshelves (or timeouts) and you're done. Streaks multiply your points. You enter your name once; only a new personal best updates the board.
