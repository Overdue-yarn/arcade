Playing as

    Knit the chart one stitch at a time. Like a real chart, work each row right to left, starting at the bottom.

    Keys: left arrow or 1 = knit (empty square), down arrow or 2 = purl (dot), up arrow or 3 = yarn over (circle), right arrow or 4 = k2tog (slash). On a phone, tap the buttons on screen.

    The first 5 rows are relaxed (15 seconds each) so you can learn the keys. After that, every row gets a little quicker, and faster rows score more. One wrong stitch ends the run. You enter your name once; only a new personal best updates the board.
