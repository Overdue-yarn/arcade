Arcade contest rules

  1. Who can play: anyone 18 or older living in the United States. Overdue Yarn Co. staff and household members can play for fun but can't win.
  2. No purchase necessary. Playing is free, and buying something doesn't improve your chances.
  3. When: the board is open . Scores saved after it closes don't count.
  4. How to win: the highest score on the board when it closes wins. If two scores tie, the one reached first wins.
  5. One spot per player: play as often as you like; the board keeps your best score. One email address per person, please.
  6. Prize: . We'll email the winner within 3 days of the board closing; if we don't hear back within 7 days, the prize goes to the next score down.
  7. Fair play: scores that look impossible, or that come from bots, cheats or modified games, will be removed. Our decision on the final board is final.
  8. Your name and email: your name (or nickname) appears on the public board. Your email stays private and is used to contact the winner. If you leave the newsletter box checked when you save a score, you'll also get The Overdue Gazette; you can unsubscribe any time.
